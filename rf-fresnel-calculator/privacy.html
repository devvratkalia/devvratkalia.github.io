Privacy Policy

RF Fresnel Calculator (com.devvratkalia.rffresnelcalculator)
Publisher: Dev_K · Effective date: 20 August 2026

This policy explains what RF Fresnel Calculator does with data. It is written in plain English, and it describes the app as it actually behaves — not a generic template.

The short version. Your calculations stay on your device. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, or location. The app does show ads, and the advertising SDK inside it collects your device's advertising identifier and basic device and ad-interaction data. You can turn ads off by subscribing to Pro.

1. Who we are

RF Fresnel Calculator is published by Dev_K. For any privacy question or request, contact wekstuff@gmail.com. For users in the EEA and UK, Dev_K is the data controller for the data described in this policy; our advertising partners act as independent controllers for the advertising data they collect.

2. What the app itself collects: nothing

The link-budget and Fresnel-zone inputs you type — frequency, distance, obstruction heights, antenna heights, units, and any saved results or preferences — are processed on your device and stored on your device. They are not uploaded to us, and we have no server that receives them.

  • No account or sign-up. We never ask for an email address, phone number, or password.
  • No location. The app does not request or use GPS or approximate location.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera.
  • No AI or cloud processing. Calculations are performed locally on the device; no input text is sent to any third-party service.

If your device or platform account has backup enabled (for example iCloud Backup or Android Auto Backup), your saved app settings may be included in that backup. That backup is controlled by Apple or Google under their own terms, not by us.

3. Advertising — what is collected and by whom

The free version of the app displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay mediation with the Unity Ads network. These SDKs run inside the app and collect data directly, whether or not you ever tap an ad.

DataWhy it is collectedShared with
Advertising identifier (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS) and app-set / vendor ID Serving and measuring ads, capping how often you see the same ad, fraud prevention Unity and mediated ad networks
Coarse device information — model, operating system version, language/locale, network type, screen size Delivering an ad that fits and works on your device; analytics Unity and mediated ad networks
Ad interactions — impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completions Advertising and measurement Unity and mediated ad networks
Diagnostics — crash logs and performance data from the SDK Keeping the ad SDK working; app stability The SDK vendor

This data is not linked to your identity by us — we do not know who you are. It is used for tracking in the sense Apple and Google define, because the advertising identifier can be used to build a profile across apps.

Your controls over ads

  • iOS: the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before it reads the IDFA. If you decline, the IDFA is not read and you will see less relevant ads. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Everywhere: subscribing to Pro removes ads, and with them the ad SDK's data collection.

Unity's own privacy policy explains what Unity does with this data: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in a region covered by the GDPR, the app shows a consent prompt from a Google-certified consent management platform before any advertising identifier is collected. Personalised advertising is used only if you consent, and you can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the app's settings. Where we rely on consent, that consent is the legal basis; for the non-advertising diagnostics needed to keep the app working, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in a functioning, stable app.

4. Subscriptions and payments

The app offers optional Pro subscriptions — pro_monthly and pro_yearly — which remove ads and unlock Pro features. Purchases are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play billing systems.

  • We never see or store your card number, billing address, or payment details.
  • The app receives only a purchase receipt or entitlement token from the store, which it uses to decide whether Pro is active. It is stored on your device.
  • Refunds, renewals, and cancellations are managed by the store, under Apple's or Google's terms and privacy policies.

5. Children

RF Fresnel Calculator is a technical tool intended for adults — RF engineers, installers, radio amateurs, and students. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age), and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you want the associated advertising identifier data removed, contact us and we will pass the request to our advertising partner.

6. How long data is kept

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. We hold no copy. Advertising data is retained by Unity and the mediated networks according to their own retention schedules, which are described in their privacy policies.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on our own systems, most requests — access, correction, deletion — are satisfied by deleting the app's data on your device or uninstalling the app.

EEA / UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise a right against the advertising data, email us and we will forward the request to the relevant ad partner, since they hold that data, not us.

California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US state laws

We do not sell personal information for money. However, sharing an advertising identifier with ad networks for cross-context behavioural advertising counts as a "sale" or "share" under the CCPA/CPRA and similar laws. You can opt out by: declining App Tracking Transparency on iOS, resetting or deleting your advertising ID on Android, or subscribing to Pro to remove ads. You may also email us to exercise your right to know, delete, or opt out; we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

8. Security

App data is protected by your device's own storage protections and passcode or biometric lock. Network traffic from the ad and billing SDKs uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No system is perfectly secure, but because the app holds no account and no server-side profile, there is very little about you to expose.

9. International transfers

Our advertising and store partners operate globally, so advertising and purchase data may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States. Those transfers are made under the safeguards those partners use, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects — for example by adding a feature that needs location or an account — we will update this page and change the effective date above before the new version ships. Material changes will also be noted in the app.

11. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or corrections: wekstuff@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.
