Orbly Support

A private, offline player for your own 180°, 360° and 3D videos.

Orbly plays the immersive videos you already have, from your camera, drone or downloads, as a sphere you can look around by moving your device or dragging. It comes with no videos of its own. Your videos stay on your device, are never uploaded, and you don't need an account.

  • 360° and 180°
  • 3D side by side and top–bottom
  • Cardboard view
  • Works offline
  • No account

Which videos play

Orbly plays equirectangular videos, the format 360° and VR180 cameras record: full 360° spheres and 180° half-spheres, flat (mono) or in 3D.

DevicePlays
iPhone and iPadMP4 and MOV files using H.264 or HEVC (H.265) video. These play best everywhere.
AndroidMP4 and MOV with H.264 or HEVC, and also MKV and WebM files through a built-in fallback decoder. How smoothly a very large or high-bitrate file plays depends on your phone.

On iPhone and iPad, files in other containers (MKV, WebM, WMV or AVI) can be added to your library but don't play yet. Tapping one shows Can't Play This File straight away, without using a free play. To watch one, convert it to MP4 with H.264 or HEVC video using a converter on your computer.

Orbly does not play copy-protected (DRM) videos from streaming services, and it does not stream from websites.

Adding videos

  1. Tap Add in your library, or Add videos on the empty library.
  2. Choose Add videos to pick one or more files, or Add a folder (Add folder on Android) to bring in every video in a folder.
  3. Pick from your device's own file picker: files on the device, on a USB drive or SD card, or in a cloud drive app that keeps a copy on the device.

Orbly remembers where each video is; it does not copy or move it. If you delete, rename or move the original file, remove it from your library and add it again. Removing a video from your library never deletes the file itself.

Search your library by name, and filter it by 180°, 360° or 3D. To hide the previews in your library, turn on Settings › Blur thumbnails.

The Format sheet

Orbly guesses each video's format from its file name, using the tags cameras and sites put there. If a video looks stretched, doubled or split, open Format in the player and choose:

Projection

360° for a full sphere, 180° for a half-sphere in front of you (VR180).

Layout

Mono for one picture, Side by side or Top–bottom for 3D, where each eye has its own half of the frame.

Tags Orbly recognises in file names include 180 or vr180 for 180°, _sbs, _lr or _3dh for side by side, and _tb, _ou or _3dv for top–bottom. Anything else starts as 360° mono. The sheet shows what was detected from the name.

Watching

  • Move your device, or drag with a finger, to look around. The compass strip at the top shows which way you face; it hides itself after a few seconds.
  • Recenter turns the view back to the video's front.
  • Tap the picture to show or hide the controls. Drag the horizon bar to move through the video, or go back 10 seconds.

Cardboard view

Tap the Cardboard view button (the glasses) to split the screen into two eyes for a phone viewer such as Google Cardboard or a similar headset. Orbly turns to landscape; put the phone in the viewer and look around with your head. 3D videos show each eye its own picture. Tap Exit Cardboard to go back.

Cardboard view needs a phone with a gyroscope. Take breaks: if you feel dizzy, sick or have eye strain, stop and rest. Don't use a viewer while walking or near hazards.

App lock

App lock is off until you turn it on. Switch it on in Settings › App lock (or on the second page when you first open Orbly), and Orbly asks for your device's own unlock when it opens:

  • iPhone and iPad: Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  • Android: your fingerprint, face unlock or screen lock PIN, pattern or password.

The check is done by the system. Orbly only learns whether it succeeded, never your face, fingerprint or passcode. If the unlock fails, use your device passcode, PIN or pattern instead.

Free plays and Premium

Without Premium, Orbly plays a limited number of videos each day for free (currently 3, reset daily). Files that can't play never use one. Premium plays any video, any time. It comes three ways, and the price is shown in your currency before you pay:

Lifetime

$19.99 once. Not a subscription: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.

Yearly

$14.99 a year, renews automatically until you cancel.

Monthly

$2.99 a month, renews automatically until you cancel.

US prices; other countries are priced by the store in local currency.

Restore purchases

Orbly has no account: your purchase belongs to your store account. On a new device, or after reinstalling, sign in to the same store account, then open Settings › Restore purchases (it is also at the foot of Go Ad-Free).

  • iPhone and iPad: use the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Android: use the same Google account you bought with on Google Play.

A purchase made on one store does not carry over to the other.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

On iPhone or iPad

In Orbly, open Settings › Manage subscription. Or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Orbly and tap Cancel Subscription.

Refunds are handled by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

On Android

In Orbly, open Settings › Manage subscription. Or open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose Orbly and tap Cancel subscription.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.

Questions

Does Orbly upload or share my videos?

No. Orbly reads your videos where they are, on your device, and never uploads them. There is no account and no analytics. See the privacy policy.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Playing your videos needs no connection. The store needs one to show prices, take a purchase or restore it.

My video looks doubled, split or stretched.

Its format was guessed wrong from the file name. Open Format in the player and pick the right projection and layout.

The view drifts or faces the wrong way.

Tap Recenter. If your device has no gyroscope, drag to look around instead.

A video I added no longer opens.

The original file was probably moved, renamed or deleted, or the drive it lives on isn't connected. Remove it from your library and add it again from its new place.

Does Orbly come with any videos?

No. Orbly is a player for videos you already have. It does not provide, sell or stream any content.
