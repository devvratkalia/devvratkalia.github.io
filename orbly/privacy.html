Orbly Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Applies to Orbly for iPhone, iPad and Android

Your videos stay on your device and are never uploaded. Orbly has no account and no analytics of its own, and we run no server that receives anything from the app. The free version shows rewarded ads: one short ad plays one video. Those ads come from ironSource Ads and Unity Ads (both run by Unity Technologies), and their software in the app sends them device and usage data so they can deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads are requested as non-personalized, Orbly never uses your advertising identifier and never asks to track you, and ads never see your videos or your library. Premium users never load an ad.

This policy explains what Orbly, made by Devvrat Kalia at Dev Studios ("we", "us"), does with information when you use the app. Orbly is a player for 180°, 360° and 3D videos that you already have. It does not include, sell or stream any content.

Your videos and your library

  • Orbly plays videos from where they already are on your device. It does not copy them to us or anyone else, and it never uploads them.
  • Your library is a list of the videos and folders you chose to add (a reference to each file or folder, and the file names). It is kept in Orbly's own storage on your device.
  • Thumbnails in your library are made on your device from a frame of each video and kept in memory only while the app is running. They are never saved to storage and never sent anywhere. With Settings › Blur thumbnails on, each preview is reduced to a soft colour wash so nobody can make out the picture.
  • Removing a video from your library removes Orbly's reference to it; it never deletes your file. Uninstalling Orbly removes everything it stored on the device.
  • On iPhone and iPad, your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include Orbly's library list and settings, as it does for other apps; the videos themselves stay where you keep them. On Android, Orbly opts out of device backup, so its library list does not leave the device.

What we collect

We, the developer, collect nothing. Orbly has no account and no sign-in, and it contains no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry of our own. We do not receive your videos, your library, your viewing, or any identifier of your device.

The ad networks in the free version do collect data, as described under Advertising. Premium users never start them.

A few things are stored on your device only, so the app works as you left it: your settings (such as App lock and Blur thumbnails), whether you have seen the welcome pages, today's count of free plays, and your Premium status as last reported by the store.

Motion sensors and file access

While a video plays, and on the first welcome page, Orbly reads your device's motion sensors so the view turns as you move. Those readings are used on the device for that moment and are not stored or sent. Orbly reaches your videos only through the system file picker, and only the files or folders you pick. It does not ask for your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location. (Ad networks may estimate your rough region, such as your country, from your IP address; see Advertising.)

App lock

App lock is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, Orbly asks the operating system to confirm it is you: Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode on iPhone and iPad, and fingerprint, face unlock or your screen lock on Android. The system does the check and tells Orbly only whether it succeeded. Orbly never sees, stores or sends your biometric data or your passcode.

Purchases

Premium (Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime) is sold by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit) on iPhone and iPad, and by Google through Google Play Billing on Android. They take the payment and hold your billing details; we never receive your name, payment card or store account details. On your device, Orbly asks the store which purchases you own so it can turn Premium on, and keeps that answer locally so the app also works offline. Apple's and Google's handling of your purchase is covered by their own privacy policies.

Advertising

Orbly is free to use with rewarded ads. When you tap a video without Premium, Orbly first tells you the reward: "Watch one short ad to play this video." If you choose to watch, one ad plays that one video; nothing carries over to the next video. If no ad can be shown (for example when you are offline or no ad is available), you can still play 3 videos a day for free. Premium removes ads.

Who serves the ads. Ads are served through LevelPlay mediation by ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, both run by Unity Technologies. Orbly does not use Google AdMob or any other ad network.

When the ad software runs. For free users, Orbly starts the ad software when the app opens (and again later if needed) so that an ad is ready when you tap a video. It is never started for Premium users.

What the ad networks receive. Their software in the app collects and sends to them, and may share with advertisers and measurement partners: identifiers for this app install and device (such as the identifier for vendors on iPhone and iPad, the App Set ID on Android, and IDs the networks create themselves); your IP address and the rough region worked out from it (not precise location); device details such as make, model, system version, language, screen size and network type; how you interact with the ads (impressions, views, taps, closes); diagnostics about ad loading and performance; and, from Unity Ads, information about in-app purchases. They use it to deliver and measure ads, to count impressions for payment, for analytics, and to detect fraud and keep ads secure.

What they do not receive. Ads never see your videos, your file names or your library, and Orbly sends nothing about what you watch.

Non-personalized, no tracking. Orbly asks the networks for non-personalized ads: before the ad software starts, it tells them that you have not given consent for personalized ads (GDPR) and that your data must not be sold or shared for cross-context advertising (CCPA). On Android, Orbly removes the advertising ID permission and tells the networks not to use the device ID. On iPhone and iPad, Orbly never asks for permission to track, so the advertising identifier is not available. Ads may still be chosen from context, such as your country, language and device type.

Their policies and your requests. How Unity handles this data, and how to ask it to access or delete data about you, is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Because we never receive ad data, we cannot look it up for you, but you can write to us and we will help you make the request.

When Orbly uses the internet

Playing your videos needs no connection. Orbly connects to the App Store or Google Play to show prices, make a purchase or restore one, and, for free users, the ad networks connect to load and show ads. Links to this website, the terms of use and support open in your browser, which has its own privacy terms.

If you write to us

If you email wekstuff@gmail.com, including through Settings › Report an ad (which opens a draft in your own mail app; nothing is sent until you send it), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you, and we will delete the conversation if you ask. Please don't send us your videos.

Children

Orbly is a general-audience video player and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we collect none ourselves. A parent or guardian with a question can write to us at the address below.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete, and we do not sell personal information. The ad networks hold their own data: you can make access or deletion requests to Unity as described in its policy (linked above), and we will help if you write to us. On iPhone and iPad you can also turn off Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking › Allow Apps to Request to Track; on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings, although Orbly does not use it. Premium stops all ads and the ad software. On your device you can remove any video from your library, change your settings, or uninstall Orbly to remove everything it stored. You may contact us with any request under privacy laws that apply to you, such as the GDPR or the CCPA, and we will answer it.

Changes to this policy

If the way Orbly handles information changes, this page and its effective date are updated before the new version is released, and a significant change is also mentioned in the release notes.
