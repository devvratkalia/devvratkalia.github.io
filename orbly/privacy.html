Orbly Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · Applies to Orbly for iPhone, iPad and Android

Your videos stay on your device and are never uploaded. Orbly has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no server that receives anything from the app. The only outside services it talks to are the App Store or Google Play, to sell and restore Premium.

This policy explains what Orbly, made by Devvrat Kalia at Dev Studios ("we", "us"), does with information when you use the app. Orbly is a player for 180°, 360° and 3D videos that you already have. It does not include, sell or stream any content.

Your videos and your library

  • Orbly plays videos from where they already are on your device. It does not copy them to us or anyone else, and it never uploads them.
  • Your library is a list of the videos and folders you chose to add (a reference to each file or folder, and the file names). It is kept in Orbly's own storage on your device.
  • Thumbnails in your library are made on your device from a frame of each video and kept in memory only while the app is running. They are never saved to storage and never sent anywhere. With Settings › Blur thumbnails on, each preview is reduced to a soft colour wash so nobody can make out the picture.
  • Removing a video from your library removes Orbly's reference to it; it never deletes your file. Uninstalling Orbly removes everything it stored on the device.
  • On iPhone and iPad, your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include Orbly's library list and settings, as it does for other apps; the videos themselves stay where you keep them. On Android, Orbly opts out of device backup, so its library list does not leave the device.

What we collect

Nothing. Orbly has no account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry, and it does not track you across apps or websites. We do not receive your videos, your library, your viewing, or any identifier of your device.

A few things are stored on your device only, so the app works as you left it: your settings (such as App lock and Blur thumbnails), whether you have seen the welcome pages, today's count of free plays, and your Premium status as last reported by the store.

Motion sensors and file access

While a video plays, and on the first welcome page, Orbly reads your device's motion sensors so the view turns as you move. Those readings are used on the device for that moment and are not stored or sent. Orbly reaches your videos only through the system file picker, and only the files or folders you pick. It does not ask for your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

App lock

App lock is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, Orbly asks the operating system to confirm it is you: Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode on iPhone and iPad, and fingerprint, face unlock or your screen lock on Android. The system does the check and tells Orbly only whether it succeeded. Orbly never sees, stores or sends your biometric data or your passcode.

Purchases

Premium (Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime) is sold by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit) on iPhone and iPad, and by Google through Google Play Billing on Android. They take the payment and hold your billing details; we never receive your name, payment card or store account details. On your device, Orbly asks the store which purchases you own so it can turn Premium on, and keeps that answer locally so the app also works offline. Apple's and Google's handling of your purchase is covered by their own privacy policies.

Advertising

The current versions of Orbly show no third-party ads. No ad network is switched on, so no advertising code runs and nothing about you or your device is sent to an ad network.

A future version may offer optional rewarded ads to free users only: watch one short ad to play one video, always your choice. If that happens, the ads would be served through ironSource LevelPlay mediation as non-personalized ads, without using your device's advertising identifier and without tracking you across apps (so Orbly would not ask for permission to track). Ad networks would still receive basic technical information needed to deliver an ad, such as device type, system version, IP address and approximate region. Ads would never see your videos or your library, and Premium users would never load an ad. We will update this policy, with its new effective date, before any version with ads is released, and describe exactly which networks are used.

When Orbly uses the internet

Playing your videos needs no connection. Orbly connects only to the App Store or Google Play to show prices, make a purchase or restore one. Links to this website, the terms of use and support open in your browser, which has its own privacy terms.

If you write to us

If you email wekstuff@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you, and we will delete the conversation if you ask. Please don't send us your videos.

Children

Orbly is a general-audience video player and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included, and the app collects none. A parent or guardian with a question can write to us at the address below.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete, and we do not sell or share personal information. On your device you can remove any video from your library, change your settings, or uninstall Orbly to remove everything it stored. You may still contact us with any request under privacy laws that apply to you, such as the GDPR or the CCPA, and we will answer it.

Changes to this policy

If the way Orbly handles information changes, this page and its effective date are updated before the new version is released, and a significant change is also mentioned in the release notes.
