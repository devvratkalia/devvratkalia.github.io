Syllabook Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026. Syllabook was called StudyMaster before version 3.0.

Syllabook is a study app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android that paces a textbook to its exam dates. It is made by Devvrat Kalia, written "we" below. This page sets out what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and the few cases in which anything reaches Apple, Google or us.

The short version

  • Syllabook collects no data. We have no server, no analytics and no advertising, and there is no account to create.
  • Your books, PDFs, recordings and questions are kept on your device, in the app's own storage.
  • Questions from your pages are set by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Syllabook sends the page to no one.
  • Apple or Google handles every payment. We never see your card or billing details.
  • On Android, Google's text-recognition component may send Google technical diagnostics when a page is read. More below.

What Syllabook keeps on your device

To plan and run your studying, the app stores:

  • your subjects, exam dates and start times, revision days and daily study hours;
  • each book's chapters, topics and printed page numbers, and the PDF of a book when you add one;
  • your key points, your questions and their answers, including questions set from your pages;
  • your attempts in checks, typed or recorded, how sure you were, the grades you chose, and your Errata;
  • your notes, typed or handwritten, and your voice recaps;
  • your settings, such as reminder times and whether questions from your pages are switched on.

All of it stays in Syllabook's storage on that device. Nothing is uploaded by the app, so we cannot read it, and we cannot recover it for you either. Delete a subject, topic, note or recording in the app and it is gone from the device. Uninstalling Syllabook removes everything it stored there.

Your device's own backup may include Syllabook's storage, as it does for other apps: iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, Time Machine or similar on a Mac, and Android's backup to your Google account, which Syllabook keeps your voice recaps and notes out of. Those backups are between you and Apple or Google.

Questions from your pages and Apple Intelligence

On an iPhone, iPad or Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, with iOS, iPadOS or macOS 26 or later, Syllabook can set up to three questions from a page of your book. To do that, it gives the text of the page you picked to Apple's model on your device. The model runs on the device; Syllabook does not use Private Cloud Compute or any other company's model, and the page goes nowhere else.

The questions it sets are shown to you first. Only the ones you keep are saved, labelled with their page and the words "set with Apple Intelligence". You can switch the feature off in Settings › Studying, and Apple Intelligence itself is controlled in your device's settings.

Report this question, on any question set this way, shows you the email first: the question, its answer, the sentence it quotes, its page, and how it was made. The email opens in your own mail app and is sent only if you send it.

Backups and course files you make

Settings › Backups writes everything to a single file, with or without your books' PDFs. A course file holds a subject's chapters, topics and notes so a teacher can pass them on; questions set from pages go into it only if the person making it chooses that, and they keep their label. You decide where these files go, whether that is Files, a folder, a message or a cloud drive. We never receive them, and whatever you send them through has its own privacy terms.

Updating from StudyMaster reads the earlier version's data on your device to bring your subjects, topics and history across. That move happens entirely within the app's storage.

Buying a membership

Memberships are sold by the App Store and Google Play, which take the payment and hold your billing details. On your device, Syllabook asks the store what you have bought and when it renews or ends, and keeps that answer locally so the app keeps working without a connection. It uses the answer only to decide which subjects are planned and to remind you before a free week ends.

What the app asks to use

Each of these is asked for when you first need it, and Syllabook works without them:

  • Camera and photos. To photograph a book's contents pages, or choose a picture of them, and read its chapters and topics. The picture is read on the device by Apple's text recognition, or Google's on Android, and it is not stored. On a Mac you choose or drop an image file instead.
  • Microphone. Only while you record a voice recap or say a check aloud. The recording is saved on the device.
  • Notifications. The day's plan in the morning, an evening note when something is still due, and the end of a block or a break. They are scheduled on the device.
  • Do Not Disturb, on Android. If you turn on Quiet while studying and allow it, Syllabook turns Do Not Disturb on while a block runs and off when it ends. It does not read your notifications.
  • Alarms and reminders, on Android. So the end of a block is announced on time.
  • Shortcuts and Siri, on iPhone and iPad. An action that starts your next block, for use in your own shortcuts. Running it opens Syllabook.

Reading pages on Android

On Android, photographed pages are read by Google's ML Kit on the phone or tablet. The picture and its text stay on the device. ML Kit itself may send Google technical diagnostics about how it is working, such as the device model and Android version, the app's name and version, performance figures and error codes, together with identifiers that Google describes as not meant to identify you. Google says it uses this to maintain ML Kit and does not share it with third parties; see Google's ML Kit data disclosure. We do not receive this information, and it does not apply on iPhone, iPad or Mac.

If you write to us

Writing to wekstuff@gmail.com, or using Settings › Help › Write to us, gives us your email address and whatever you choose to write; the draft the app starts names the app and its version in the subject line. We use it to answer you and for nothing else, and we will delete the conversation if you ask.

Children

Syllabook is meant for students aged 13 and over. It collects nothing from anyone, children included. A parent or guardian with a question can write to the address above.

Your choices

Because nothing about you is held on our side, there is nothing for us to show, correct, export or delete. On your device you can change or delete anything in the app, save a backup file, switch off questions from your pages, or uninstall Syllabook.

When this policy changes

If the way Syllabook handles information changes, this page and its date are updated before the new version arrives, and a significant change is also mentioned in the release notes.
