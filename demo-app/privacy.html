Privacy Policy for Focus Timer

App: Focus Timer (com.devvratkalia.focustimer)
Publisher: Dev_K
Contact: wekstuff@gmail.com
Effective date: 31 July 2026

Focus Timer is a minimalist pomodoro timer with ambient sounds and streak tracking, made mainly for students. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app collects, who it goes to, and what you can do about it.

Two things are worth saying up front:

  • Your timer data stays on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours that stores your sessions or streaks.
  • The app shows ads, and ads mean identifiers. The advertising SDK collects your device's advertising ID and related device information and shares it with ad networks, including for tracking and personalised advertising. That happens whether or not you ever tap an ad.

1. Who is responsible

Focus Timer is published by Dev_K, an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, email wekstuff@gmail.com. That address is the single point of contact for this app, and it is where you should send access, deletion, or opt-out requests.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is created by you and kept in the app's local storage on your phone or tablet. It is not uploaded to us and we cannot see it:

  • Timer settings — focus and break lengths, number of rounds, ambient sound choice, volume.
  • Session history and streaks — completed pomodoros, dates, and the streak counter derived from them.
  • App preferences — theme, notification and sound toggles, and whether you have dismissed onboarding or consent screens.

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because this data lives only on your device, uninstalling deletes it permanently — there is no cloud backup on our side to restore it from, though your operating system's own device backup may include it if you have that turned on.

3. Advertising — what the ad SDKs collect

Focus Timer uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with the Unity Ads adapter to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Mediation means LevelPlay may pass an ad request to other advertising networks in its network list to fill the slot.

When ads are active, the following is collected from your device by the ad SDKs and shared with LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and the mediated ad networks that participate in filling an ad slot:

None of this is linked by us to a name, email address, or account, because the app has no accounts. It is nevertheless device-level data, and advertising identifiers are designed to be persistent across apps until you reset them — see section 8 for how.

Location

Focus Timer does not ask for device location permission and does not collect GPS or precise location. Ad networks receiving an ad request can still infer an approximate location (roughly city or region level) from your IP address and network information, as is standard for internet advertising.

Ad partner policies

Once data reaches an ad partner, that partner is the controller of it and handles it under its own policy. The primary partners are Unity (LevelPlay mediation and Unity Ads) and the advertising networks Unity mediates. Their privacy policies govern what they do with the data, including how long they keep it.

4. Consent for ads in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message from a certified consent management platform before advertising identifiers are collected for personalised advertising. Your choice is passed to the advertising SDKs, and the legal basis for that processing is your consent. You can change your choice later from the privacy or settings screen inside the app. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised or contextual, and the SDKs will limit what they collect accordingly.

5. Tracking permission on iOS

On iOS, the app presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before it reads the IDFA. If you decline, the app does not access the IDFA and ads become non-personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. Pro subscriptions

Focus Timer offers optional subscriptions — pro_monthly and pro_yearly. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store, depending on where you installed the app.

  • We never see or receive your card number, bank details, or billing address. The store handles payment.
  • The app receives only a purchase receipt or entitlement status from the store so it can tell whether your subscription is currently active.
  • The store keeps its own record of the transaction under its own privacy policy, and provides us with aggregate, non-identifying sales reporting.

Buying a subscription does not create an account with us and does not give us your email address.

7. What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no password, no email collection inside the app.
  • No contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or camera access.
  • No location permission request.
  • No sending of your notes, session data, or any content to AI services — the app has no AI features enabled.
  • No selling of personal information for money.

8. Your choices and controls

Limit or reset the advertising identifier

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset the advertising ID. Deleting it stops apps from receiving it.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off tracking for Focus Timer or for all apps.

Delete your data

Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. That removes everything the app stores locally. For identifiers already collected by ad partners, reset your advertising ID as above and, if you want, contact the relevant ad network directly.

Withdraw ad consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland)

Reopen the consent screen from the app's privacy or settings screen and change your answer.

9. Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that already happened. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Practical note, so you know what to expect: because the app has no accounts and stores nothing about you on our side, we usually cannot look you up. If you email wekstuff@gmail.com with a request, we will respond within 30 days and either action it or explain honestly that the data in question is on your device (where you control it) or held by an ad partner (whom we will identify so you can approach them directly).

Legal bases used: consent for personalised advertising and advertising identifiers where consent is required; legitimate interests for fraud prevention, crash diagnostics, and keeping the app functioning; contract for delivering a subscription you have purchased.

10. Your rights under CCPA/CPRA (California) and similar US state laws

We do not sell personal information for money. However, sharing device and advertising identifiers with ad networks for personalised advertising counts as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law, and as "targeted advertising" under several other US state privacy laws. You have the right to opt out of that.

To opt out: reset or delete your advertising ID (Android), turn off tracking for this app (iOS), or email wekstuff@gmail.com with "Opt out of sharing" in the subject line. The app also honours Global Privacy Control signals where the advertising SDKs support them.

The categories involved are identifiers (advertising and device IDs) and internet or other electronic network activity (ad interactions, diagnostics). We do not collect sensitive personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

11. Children

Focus Timer is intended for students of secondary-school age and above and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). The app is not configured as a child-directed service, and its ads are not child-directed. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under those ages. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the app, email wekstuff@gmail.com and we will help you clear the data and advise on resetting the advertising identifier.

12. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data held by ad networks and the app stores is retained under their own schedules, which we do not control. We hold no personal data on our own servers, so there is nothing on our side to retain or delete.

13. International transfers

The advertising and store partners are global companies and may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, they rely on approved transfer mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's own security — screen lock, OS sandboxing, and storage encryption. Network calls made by the app and its SDKs use encrypted HTTPS connections. No system is perfectly secure, but because the app holds no account credentials and no payment data, the exposure from any single device is limited to your own timer settings and streak history.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who it is shared with, the effective date at the top will change and the updated policy will be published here before the change takes effect in the app. Material changes affecting advertising consent will also re-prompt you inside the app where the law requires it.

16. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: wekstuff@gmail.com. Please include the app name (Focus Timer) and your platform (Android or iOS) so we can answer accurately.
