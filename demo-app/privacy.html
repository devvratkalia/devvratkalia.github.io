Privacy Policy for Focus Timer

App: Focus Timer (com.devvratkalia.focustimer)
Publisher: Dev_K
Contact: wekstuff@gmail.com
Effective date: 9 August 2026

Focus Timer is a minimalist pomodoro timer with ambient sounds and streak tracking, made mainly for students. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app collects, who it goes to, and what you can do about it.

Three things are worth saying up front:

  • Your timer data stays on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours that stores your sessions or streaks.
  • The free app shows ads, and ads mean identifiers. The advertising SDK collects your device's advertising ID and related device information and shares it with ad networks, including for tracking and personalised advertising. That happens whether or not you ever tap an ad.
  • There is now a paid Pro subscription. It is billed by Google Play or Apple, not by us. We never see your card, and we never receive your name or billing address — only the entitlement status the store reports back to the app. Pro removes ads, and with ads off the collection described in section 3 does not take place.

1. Who is responsible

Focus Timer is published by Dev_K, an independent developer. For any privacy question or request, email wekstuff@gmail.com. That address is the single point of contact for this app, and it is where you should send access, deletion, or opt-out requests.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is created by you and kept in the app's local storage on your phone or tablet. It is not uploaded to us and we cannot see it:

  • Timer settings — focus and break lengths, number of rounds, ambient sound choice, volume.
  • Session history and streaks — completed pomodoros, dates, and the streak counter derived from them.
  • App preferences — theme, notification and sound toggles, and whether you have dismissed onboarding or consent screens.
  • Your Pro entitlement flag and the app-lock setting described in section 5, held in your device's encrypted keystore (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because this data lives only on your device, uninstalling deletes it permanently — there is no cloud backup on our side to restore it from, though your operating system's own device backup may include it if you have that turned on. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription; see section 4.

3. Advertising — what the ad SDKs collect

Focus Timer uses Unity LevelPlay mediation with the Unity Ads adapter to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Mediation means LevelPlay may pass an ad request to other advertising networks in its network list to fill the slot.

When ads are active, the following is collected from your device by the ad SDKs and shared with LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and the mediated ad networks that participate in filling an ad slot:

None of this is linked by us to a name, email address, or account, because the app has no accounts. It is nevertheless device-level data, and advertising identifiers are designed to be persistent across apps until you reset them — see section 8 for how.

Ads and the Pro subscription

Ads are how the free version pays for itself. While you hold an active Pro subscription, the app does not show ads: the advertising SDK is not started, so the collection and sharing set out in the table above does not happen for that period. If your subscription is cancelled or lapses, the app returns to the ad-supported mode and this section applies again.

Location

Focus Timer does not ask for device location permission and does not collect GPS or precise location. Ad networks receiving an ad request can still infer an approximate location (roughly city or region level) from your IP address and network information, as is standard for internet advertising.

Ad partner policies

Once data reaches an ad partner, that partner is the controller of it and handles it under its own policy. The primary partners are Unity (LevelPlay mediation and the Unity Ads network) and the demand networks LevelPlay mediates to. Unity's privacy policy is at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy, and Unity's list of the ad partners it works with is published alongside it. We do not control what those partners do with data after it leaves the device, and we have no ability to reach into their systems to retrieve or delete it on your behalf — requests of that kind have to go to them, though we will point you to the right contact if you email us.

4. Pro subscription and purchases

Focus Timer offers optional subscriptions — a monthly plan and a yearly plan — that remove ads and unlock Pro features. Payment is handled entirely by the store you installed the app from: Google Play Billing on Android, the App Store on iOS. We are not a payment processor and there is no checkout of ours.

  • We never receive your card or bank details, your billing address, or your store account name or email.
  • We do receive, through the store's billing API, an anonymous purchase token or receipt, the product identifier of the plan, and whether the subscription is currently active, expired, cancelled, or in a grace or billing-retry period. The app uses this only to decide whether to unlock Pro and hide ads.
  • Stored on your device is the resulting entitlement state, kept in the encrypted keystore described in section 5. Restoring purchases on a new device re-queries the store; it does not move any of your timer history.

Google and Apple process your payment as independent controllers under their own privacy policies, and they retain purchase records for tax and accounting reasons regardless of what we do. Cancellations and refunds are handled in your store account — see the Terms of Use.

5. App lock, biometrics, and secure storage

Focus Timer includes an optional app lock. If you turn it on, the app asks your device to verify you with the biometric or device-credential check you already use — fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern, or passcode.

That check is performed by your operating system, not by us. The app never sees, receives, or stores your fingerprint, face data, PIN, or passcode. It receives only a pass-or-fail result from the OS, and there is no biometric template anywhere in the app or in any transmission it makes.

Settings that should not sit in plain storage — the app-lock preference and the Pro entitlement state — are held with the platform's encrypted secret storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore). Everything in this section stays on the device.

6. Notifications, reviews, and links

  • Notifications and permissions. The app asks for a permission only when a feature you use needs one — for example notification permission for timer alerts. If you decline, that feature is skipped and nothing is collected in its place.
  • Rating prompt. The app can show your store's built-in in-app review prompt. The review sheet is provided and handled by Google or Apple; we do not see whether you rated, what you wrote, or that the prompt appeared.
  • Outbound links. Tapping a link — this policy, the Terms, a support email — hands off to your browser or mail app. Once you leave the app, that destination's own policy applies.
  • App version information. The app reads its own version and build number locally, to display it in settings and to help with support emails you choose to send us.

7. Children

Focus Timer is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect data from them, and the app is not configured as a child-directed service for advertising purposes. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, email wekstuff@gmail.com and we will help — though, as the app holds no account and no server-side records, in practice clearing app storage or uninstalling removes everything that exists.

8. Your choices and controls

  • Reset or delete the advertising ID. On Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. On iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Refuse app tracking (iOS). The app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before any IDFA is read. Saying no means the IDFA is not read; ads still appear, but non-personalised. You can change the answer later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). Where consent is required, a consent prompt appears before advertising identifiers are collected, and your choices are passed to the ad networks. You can reopen and change those choices from the app's privacy settings.
  • Remove ads entirely. Subscribing to Pro turns the advertising SDK off, as described in section 3.
  • Turn off the app lock. The app-lock toggle is in the app's settings, and turning it off deletes the stored preference.
  • Delete everything. Clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information or of targeted advertising. Email wekstuff@gmail.com to exercise any of them and we will respond within the period the applicable law requires.

Two honest caveats. First, we hold no account and no server-side profile, so for most requests the only data that exists is on your device and under your control. Second, sharing advertising identifiers with ad networks for personalised advertising counts as "sharing" for targeted advertising under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act; the way to opt out of it in this app is to withhold the tracking or consent permission, reset your advertising ID, or subscribe to Pro. Where we rely on a legal basis under the UK/EU GDPR, it is your consent for advertising identifiers, and our legitimate interest in a working, secure app for on-device functionality and diagnostics.

10. Retention and security

We keep nothing on our own servers, because there are none in this app's path. On-device data lasts until you clear it or uninstall. Data collected by the ad SDKs and by the app stores is retained by those companies under their own schedules, which we neither set nor control. Within the app, secrets are held in the platform keystore and network calls made by the SDKs use encrypted connections; no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.
