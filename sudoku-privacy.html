Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

Welcome to our Sudoku App. This Privacy Policy explains how developer Devvrat Kalia ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information like your name or phone number to play Sudoku. However, we do automatically collect certain non-personal data through our third-party partners to provide core game features and display advertisements.

2. App Tracking & Advertisements

App Tracking Transparency (ATT) & Personalized Ads

Our app utilizes third-party advertising networks that track your activity across apps and websites to deliver personalized advertisements. When you open the app for the first time, you will see a prompt asking for permission to track your activity.

If you consent, we use industry-standard advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID) to show ads tailored to your interests. If you opt-out, you will still see ads, but they will be generic and less relevant to you.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Our app integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect data used to identify your device, track crashes, and serve ads. These services include:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics: To monitor app performance, fix bugs, and understand player engagement. Firebase Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention and Security

Since we do not collect personal identifiers on our own servers, we do not store personal details. Third-party ad networks and analytics platforms retain data according to their own established privacy policies and strict security standards.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Sudoku app is enjoyable for all ages. However, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we immediately take steps to delete it from our systems.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top.
