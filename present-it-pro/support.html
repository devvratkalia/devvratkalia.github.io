Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start and stop drawing on my screen?

Press ⌘ ⇧ A anywhere to activate drawing mode. You can also click the Draw button in the Floating HUD or in the Menu Bar icon. To exit drawing mode, simply press Esc or ⌘ ⇧ A again.

How do I reposition or collapse the Floating HUD?

Click and drag the horizontal grab handle (≡) at the left of the toolbar to place it anywhere on your screen. To minimize the HUD into a compact pill, click the left chevron icon (◂). Click the right chevron (▸) to expand it back.

How does the Spotlight feature work?

Press ⌘ ⇧ S to toggle the Spotlight. The background will smoothly dim while a bright, circular aperture follows your mouse cursor. In the Preferences window, you can customize the beam radius, edge softness, and ambient dimming intensity.

Why does macOS request Accessibility permissions?

macOS requires Accessibility permissions so the application can detect mouse movement coordinates across third-party applications and trigger click animations in real-time. We never log, record, or transmit mouse events.

Does Present IT Pro support multiple external monitors?

Yes! Present IT Pro automatically detects all connected displays, projector outputs, and Sidecar iPads, provisioning transparent annotation and spotlight overlays across each screen simultaneously.

How do I copy my drawings to the clipboard?

Click the camera icon on the Floating HUD or choose Copy Canvas Snapshot from the Menu Bar menu. The current annotation overlay is instantly copied to your macOS clipboard as a high-resolution PNG ready to paste into Slack, Keynote, or Notion.

System Requirements

  • macOS Version: macOS Sonoma 14.0 or macOS Sequoia 15.0+
  • Hardware: Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) or Intel-based Mac
  • Architecture: Universal macOS Binary
