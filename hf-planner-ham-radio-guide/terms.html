1. What the App is

The App is a planning aid for amateur HF radio. It combines the locations and times you enter with solar-cycle, space-weather and weather data to estimate which HF frequencies are likely to work on a given path, in 15-minute steps. It produces a frequency matrix, an exportable PDF report for a chosen period, and descriptive RF pattern charts.

It predicts; it does not promise. HF propagation depends on ionospheric conditions that are modelled statistically and forecast imperfectly. Predictions, charts, and reports produced by the App are estimates and will sometimes be wrong.
