This policy explains what the HF Planner : Ham Radio guide app (the “App”) does with data. It is written in plain English, and it is meant to be accurate rather than reassuring: the App shows ads, and ad software collects device identifiers. That is described in full below.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never create a login, and we never ask for your name, email, or callsign.
  • Your planning inputs and generated reports stay on your device. We do not upload them to a server we control.
  • The App shows ads through Unity LevelPlay mediation with Unity Ads. Advertising software collects device and advertising identifiers, coarse device information, crash diagnostics, and ad interaction data, and shares it with advertising partners for advertising and tracking.
  • Optional Pro subscriptions (pro_monthly, pro_yearly) are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • The App can be locked with your fingerprint, face, or device passcode. That check is performed by your operating system; we never receive your biometric data.
  • The App does not contain any AI feature and sends nothing you type to an AI provider.
  • The App is not directed to children.
  • Questions, requests, complaints: wekstuff@gmail.com.

Dev_K (“we”, “us”) publishes the App and is the data controller for the processing described here, for the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: wekstuff@gmail.com.

Note that the advertising networks reached through mediation act as controllers of the data they collect for their own advertising purposes. We do not control what they do with it beyond the settings and consent signals we pass to them.

2. What the App does with the information you enter

The App is an HF propagation planning tool. To produce a plan, you enter or select things like transmit and receive locations, the time window you want to plan for, and station parameters. The App combines those inputs with solar-cycle, space-weather and weather data to produce a 15-minute frequency matrix, a PDF report, and RF pattern charts.

  • Your planning inputs, saved plans, settings and generated PDF reports are stored locally on your device. We do not have a user database and cannot read them.
  • A PDF or chart leaves your device only when you choose to share, export, print, or email it. What happens to it after that is governed by whatever app or service you send it to.
  • To fetch solar, space-weather and weather data, the App makes requests over the internet. Such a request necessarily discloses your device’s IP address and the parameters of the request (for example the coordinates and time range you are planning for) to the data source and to the networks in between. We do not attach an identifier or profile to those requests.
  • The map view loads map tiles from a third-party map tile service. Loading a tile discloses your device’s IP address and the area of the map you are looking at to that service, on the same terms as any other web request. Tiles are cached on your device.
  • If you allow the App to use your device location to fill in a station position, that position is used on the device for the calculation and is not sent to advertising partners. You can refuse or revoke the permission in your operating system settings and enter coordinates manually instead.

4. Consent, and how to limit ad tracking

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through a certified consent management platform before advertising identifiers are collected for personalised advertising. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the privacy or settings screen in the App.
  • On iOS, the App must ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before the IDFA can be read. If you decline, no IDFA is read. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation there.
  • Turning off personalisation does not remove ads; it makes them less targeted.
  • Buying a Pro subscription removes ads going forward. It does not delete data that ad networks collected earlier — for that, contact the network or use the controls above.

7. App lock, biometrics and secure storage

  • If you enable the app lock, the App asks your operating system to verify you with your fingerprint, face, or device passcode. The comparison happens inside your device’s secure hardware. Your fingerprint or face template is never given to the App, never leaves your device, and is never sent to us or to anyone else. The App receives only a yes-or-no answer.
  • Sensitive values the App needs to keep (such as the app-lock preference and any stored keys) are held in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore-backed encrypted store, not in plain files.
  • The app lock protects the App on an unlocked device. It is not full-disk encryption and it does not protect against someone who controls your device credential or a rooted/jailbroken system.

8. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data, and we have no user database to sell. Data leaves your device only to:

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource, Unity Ads and the ad networks they mediate — the data in section 3, for advertising and tracking.

  • Google Play or Apple — purchase, subscription and review handling, and crash reporting where you have opted into it at the operating-system level.
  • Anyone you send a report to — only when you choose to share, print or export it.
