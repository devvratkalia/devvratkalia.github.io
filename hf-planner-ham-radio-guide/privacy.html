This policy explains what the HF Planner : Ham Radio guide app (the “App”) does with data. It is written in plain English, and it is meant to be accurate rather than reassuring: the App shows ads, and ad software collects device identifiers. That is described in full below.

The short version

2. What the App does with the information you enter

The App is an HF propagation planning tool. To produce a plan, you enter or select things like transmit and receive locations, the time window you want to plan for, and station parameters. The App combines those inputs with solar-cycle, space-weather and weather data to produce a 15-minute frequency matrix, a PDF report, and RF pattern charts.

  • Your planning inputs, saved plans, settings and generated PDF reports are stored locally on your device. We do not have a user database and cannot read them.
  • A PDF or chart leaves your device only when you choose to share, export, print, or email it. What happens to it after that is governed by whatever app or service you send it to.
  • To fetch solar, space-weather and weather data, the App makes requests over the internet. Such a request necessarily discloses your device’s IP address and the parameters of the request (for example the coordinates and time range you are planning for) to the data source and to the networks in between. We do not attach an identifier or profile to those requests.
  • If you allow the App to use your device location to fill in a station position, that position is used on the device for the calculation and is not sent to advertising partners. You can refuse or revoke the permission in your operating system settings and enter coordinates manually instead.

4. Consent and your ad controls

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland

Before any advertising identifier is read, the App shows a consent message from a certified consent management platform. Identifiers are only collected for advertising if you consent. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the privacy or consent option in the App’s settings; withdrawal applies going forward and does not undo processing that already happened.

If you are on iOS

The App asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before the IDFA is read. If you decline, the IDFA is not accessed and you will see less relevant ads rather than no ads. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On any device

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising turns off personalised ads.
  • Subscribing to Pro removes ads, which stops the collection described in section 3.
  • Uninstalling the App stops all collection and deletes the data stored on your device.
