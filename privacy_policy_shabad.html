Privacy Policy for Shabad

Last Updated: October 29, 2026 • App Version 3.0.2 (Build 18)

This Privacy Policy applies to the Shabad (also referred to as Shabd) mobile game application (the "App"), developed by Devvrat Kalia ("we", "our", or "us").

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data collected, used, or shared when you play our game. The App does not require any account registration or login. This policy has been updated to fully comply with Google Play Developer Program Policies, specifically the Google Play User Data Policy and the Google Play Data Safety requirements.

Key Privacy Guarantees:
  • No Firebase Integration: The App does not use any Firebase SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Auth, or Firestore).
  • No User Accounts: The App does not support user account creation or login. We never collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email, physical address, or phone number.
  • Local Game Storage: All gameplay progress, best times, streaks, diamonds and your game language are stored strictly locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore.
  • Word Data on Your Device: Word lists and word meanings are bundled inside the App (derived from Wiktionary, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0). Looking up a meaning happens entirely on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

1. Information We Collect, Share, and Process

Our App integrates reliable third-party SDKs for advertising mediation, monetization, and in-app commerce. Depending on your device permissions and settings, the following categories of data are processed:

1.1 Location Data (Approximate Location)

  • Data Collected & Shared: Approximate location (general geographic area such as country or city derived from your device's IP address). The App does not request GPS location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) and never accesses precise geolocation.
  • Purposes: Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention, security, and compliance.
  • Sharing: Shared with third-party advertising and mediation partners (such as Unity Ads and Unity LevelPlay).

1.2 Device or Other Identifiers

  • Data Collected & Shared: Google Play Services Advertising ID (AAID / com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), device make and model, operating system version, network connection type, system locale, and device memory status.
  • Purposes: Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention, security, and compliance, App functionality.
  • Sharing: Shared with ad mediation networks (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads) for ad delivery, attribution, and frequency capping.

1.3 App Activity and Interactions

  • Data Collected & Shared: In-app ad interactions (impressions, clicks, completed rewarded video views), gameplay state transitions, and reward unlocks.
  • Purposes: Advertising or marketing, Analytics, App functionality.
  • Sharing: Ad interaction metrics are shared with ad networks to confirm reward grants (e.g. diamonds for a watched video) and deliver contextual ads.

1.4 Financial Info (Purchase History)

  • Data Collected: Transaction status and in-app purchase tokens for digital goods (consumable diamond packs, and the one-time Remove Ads purchase and bundles). The App has no subscriptions.
  • Payment Protection: All payment transactions are processed entirely and securely by Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC). We never collect, store, or have access to your credit card, debit card, bank account, or payment instrument credentials.
  • Collection Type: Optional (only collected when a user chooses to make an in-app purchase).
  • Purposes: App functionality (fulfilling purchases, restoring entitlements), Analytics, Fraud prevention.

1.5 App Info, Performance, and Diagnostics

  • Data Collected & Shared: Crash logs, application responsiveness (ANR logs), diagnostic reports, and ad SDK operational performance metrics.
  • Purposes: Analytics, App functionality, Fraud prevention, security, and compliance.
  • Sharing: Unity LevelPlay Ad Quality SDK, Google Play Services.

Your Privacy Choices (Consent)

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Before any ad is requested, the App asks whether ads may be personalised using your device's advertising ID. No ad is loaded until you answer. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised.
  • United States: Settings > Privacy choices has a "Do not sell or share my info" switch that opts you out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Passing your choice on: Your choice is stored only on your device, applied before the ad SDK starts, and passed to our mediation partner Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with.
  • Changing your mind: Open Settings > Privacy choices in the App at any time to change your answer.

4. Data Security and Encryption

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the App and our third-party services is protected using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3 / HTTPS) encryption protocols.
  • Local Storage: Gameplay progress, diamonds and preferences are stored purely locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore. Diamonds are not backed up to any server; one-time purchases such as Remove Ads can be restored through Google Play.

5. Data Retention and Deletion Request Mechanism

Your Right to Data Deletion:
Because the App stores your gameplay progress purely on your local device, you can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the App's storage:
  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Tap Apps > Shabad (or Shabd).
  3. Tap Storage & cache > tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).
  4. Alternatively, uninstalling the App will immediately and permanently erase all local game data, including your diamond balance.

Advertising Identifier Reset: To reset or delete your Google Advertising ID (AAID), visit Android Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

Third-Party Deletion Requests: For inquiries regarding data processed by third parties:

  • To delete data processed by Unity Ads / LevelPlay, email Unity's Data Protection Officer at DPO@unity3d.com.
  • To manage or delete Google data, review Google's Privacy Policy.

For any direct questions regarding data deletion, contact us at kalia.devvrat@gmail.com (or wekstuff@gmail.com) with the subject line Shabad - Data Deletion Request.
