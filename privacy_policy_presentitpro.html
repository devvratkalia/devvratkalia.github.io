Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Present IT Pro ("the Application"), developed by Devvrat Kalia ("we", "us", or "our"), is designed with an uncompromising commitment to user privacy. We believe your screen, presentations, and working environments belong exclusively to you.

1. Complete On-Device Processing

Present IT Pro runs 100% locally on your Mac. The application does not communicate with external servers, cloud databases, or third-party tracking services. There are no user accounts, no telemetry frameworks, and no advertising trackers integrated into the app.

2. Screen Capture & Accessibility Permissions

To deliver live screen annotations, cursor spotlight focusing, and click ripple animations, macOS requires standard system permissions:

  • Screen Recording / Overlay Access: Used solely in volatile GPU memory to draw annotations on top of your screen windows and to render the focused spotlight beam. No video or image frames are ever recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted over the internet.
  • Accessibility / Mouse Monitoring: Used exclusively to detect mouse cursor coordinates and click events in real-time so the spotlight beam and cursor halo can follow your pointer smoothly. Mouse events are never logged or stored.

3. Clipboard & Snapshot Feature

When you explicitly click the "Copy Snapshot to Clipboard" button (or use its shortcut), the application renders an image of your active drawing layer directly into your local macOS pasteboard (NSPasteboard). This data remains on your machine and is never sent to any remote server.

4. Data Collection & Analytics

We collect zero personal data. Specifically:

  • We do NOT collect your name, email, IP address, or location.
  • We do NOT track how often you use the app or which tools you select.
  • We do NOT use third-party analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, no Firebase, no Mixpanel).

5. Crash Logs & Apple Diagnostics

If you choose to share crash reports with developers through macOS or App Store Connect, Apple provides anonymized diagnostic crash logs in accordance with Apple's standard Privacy Policy. These logs contain only technical stack traces and device model information to help us fix bugs.

6. Children's Privacy

Present IT Pro does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted immediately on this webpage with a revised revision date.
