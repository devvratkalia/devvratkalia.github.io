Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Summary: 100% On-Device Privacy

WebScraperAI Studio is engineered from the ground up to respect user privacy. We do not operate remote servers, tracking proxies, cloud telemetry, or third-party analytics infrastructure. All webpage rendering, natural language parsing, and dataset extractions occur strictly on your local Mac.

2. Zero Telemetry & Zero Analytics

WebScraperAI Studio does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, browsing history, URLs entered, extracted datasets, or hardware identifiers. There are zero third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or crash tracking beacons included in the app.

3. App Sandbox Compliance

The application runs strictly enclosed within macOS App Sandbox restrictions. Network requests are made solely to the URLs explicitly entered by the user in the address bar. Files are saved only to directories chosen explicitly via system save dialogs.

4. Mac App Store Licensing

WebScraperAI Studio is distributed as an upfront paid application through the Mac App Store. License verification is handled directly by macOS and Apple. The developer does not receive, view, or process your credit card, Apple ID credentials, or billing details.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer directly at wekstuff@gmail.com.
